The Guided Journey

Click through the four stages of how every match is handled.

01. Comprehensive 16-Question Questionnaire

You join by answering our thoughtful 16-point questionnaire covering lifestyle, values, intellectual interests, and explicit deal-breakers.

  • No public directory where anyone can screenshot or browse your details.
  • Evaluated manually on intent, communication style, and life goals.
  • If there is no genuine compatible match right now, we keep your spot on standby instead of forcing bad dates.

02. Limited-Profile Mutual Consent

When a potential match is identified, both members receive a carefully anonymized snapshot of the other person.

  • See only age, city, profession, lifestyle habits, and core interests.
  • Zero contact details are shown: no phone numbers, social handles, or email.
  • Both individuals must independently tap "Interested" before an introduction proceeds.

03. Two Guided Virtual Meetings

Instead of getting lost in weeks of dry texting, mutual matches meet through structured, low-pressure virtual dates.

  • Scheduled via private Google Meet links (audio or video based on comfort).
  • Date #1 tests conversational rapport; Date #2 confirms authentic comfort.
  • Founder-moderated or scheduled assistance to keep boundaries safe and respectful.

04. Admin-Controlled Chat Unlock

Private text, voice notes, and calls unlock inside the platform only after both individuals confirm they feel comfortable.

  • Direct messaging remains locked during early stages to avoid harassment and unwanted advances.
  • Once mutual trust is established, direct messaging is enabled.
  • Exchange personal phone numbers and socials at your own pace, on your own terms.
